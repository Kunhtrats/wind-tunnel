Physics & limits

D2Q9 BGK lattice Boltzmann approximates low-Mach Navier–Stokes:

∂ρ/∂t + ∇·(ρu) = 0
∂u/∂t + u·∇u = −∇p/ρ + ν∇²u
p = ρ/3; ν = (τ − ½)/3

No-slip bounce-back walls. Tunnel mode uses a velocity inlet and extrapolated outlet. Fan mode uses a local momentum actuator with open left/right edges, not a mass source or a resolved rotating fan. Pressure is relative to the right outlet.

∂T/∂t + u·∇T = α∇²T
α = ν/0.71

Passive temperature: 20 °C inlet and tunnel walls, heated obstacles. No buoyancy, compression heating or thermal feedback. Bernoulli applies along steady inviscid streamlines, not throughout wakes.

Lattice units: length lu, time ts. Reynolds number uses H/4 as reference length. This low-Re, 2D laminar model is educational, not full-scale air CFD. Coandă attachment is an outcome, not guaranteed. Engineering use requires grid convergence and validation; resolution changes also change Re at fixed lattice parameters.

Reading the experiment

Wind speed: low values give weaker motion; high values increase inertia and Reynolds number. Above 0.06 lu/ts, contractions may hit the low-Mach safety limit. A stopped fan stops adding momentum; the existing flow takes time to decay.

Simulation pace: 1× spends less time computing; 8× computes more physical timesteps per update. Actual speed depends on your device and grid. It does not multiply particle velocity or change the equations.

Viscosity: low values diffuse momentum less and allow sharper shear and longer-lived wakes. High values damp motion more and lower Reynolds number. In this model thermal diffusivity also rises with viscosity (α = ν/0.71).

Incidence: negative and positive angles tilt the section in opposite directions. Greater magnitude usually increases deflection and separation. Angle of attack is relative to the local incoming wind, so it also depends on fan direction. Live edits preserve the field but reinitialize changed boundary cells; this is not a validated moving-wing load model.

Surface temperature: below 20 °C cools the nearby flow; above 20 °C heats it. Temperature is passive: it changes the heat map, not density, lift, buoyancy or wind speed.

Fan and resolution: the fan accelerates nearby fluid in its arrow direction. Use tunnel mode for a full-width inlet or the Coandă preset slot. Smooth outlines are supersampled visually; Ultra also refines the actual fluid grid at higher cost. Grid-based no-slip boundaries remain approximate.

Physical units: km/h is a conversion using your chosen length/time scales; ν in m²/s = ν in lu²/ts × (metres/cell)² ÷ (seconds/step). Changing units or scale does not change the lattice flow or establish realistic air viscosity.
